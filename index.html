About

I am a Research Fellow in the College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS) at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. My postdoctoral supervisor is Professor Dacheng Tao. I received both my bachelor’s degree and my PhD from Beihang University, where Professor Wenjun Wu was my doctoral advisor.

My current research focuses on reasoning dynamics in foundation models and embodied multi-agent systems. My earlier work includes graph data mining and intelligent education.

Research

I study how reasoning changes as information becomes available, from intermediate states in foundation models to joint plans in robot teams.
